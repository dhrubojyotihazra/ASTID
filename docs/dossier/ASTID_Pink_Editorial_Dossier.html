1.1 Plain English Translation: What Exactly is ASTID?

ASTID is not a consumer payment app, and it is not a commercial SaaS product with billing. Everyday bank customers never open ASTID to make a payment. Instead, ASTID is the internal risk-operations mission-control console used by a bank's fraud investigation team.

In commercial banking, when a customer initiates a digital payment, ASTID scores the event in the background in under 80 milliseconds. Legitimate payments complete silently with zero friction. If a suspicious pattern emerges (such as an account takeover, sudden velocity burst, or money laundering mule transfer), ASTID routes the transaction to an internal review queue with plain-English Whitebox Risk Insights badges so bank officers can inspect, confirm, or freeze the transfer before funds leave the institution.

🏦 Real-World Analogy (Stripe Radar & Razorpay Thirdwatch)
ASTID occupies the exact operational position that Stripe Radar occupies for Stripe merchants, that Razorpay Thirdwatch occupies for Indian retail e-commerce merchants, and that Feedzai Pulse occupies for tier-1 commercial banking risk departments.

03 Mathematical Formalisms & Algorithmic Modeling

3.1 PaySim Data Leakage Proof (The errorBalanceDest Mathematical Trap)

In early baseline prototypes and common online Kaggle implementations, researchers frequently compute post-transaction balance identities:

$$\text{errorBalanceDest} = \text{oldbalanceDest} + \text{amount} - \text{newbalanceDest}$$

When passed to a gradient-boosted tree, this feature yields an artificial \(\text{PR-AUC} = 1.0000\). This represents a catastrophic target/lookahead leakage:

  • In PaySim's synthetic agent simulator, fraudulent cash-outs evacuated the sender's account but left destination balances uncredited (\(\text{newbalanceDest} = 0.0\)).
  • Consequently, for fraud, \(\text{errorBalanceDest} \equiv \text{amount}\), exposing the simulator's internal heuristics.
  • In real-world pre-authorization scoring, recipient post-transaction ledger balances (\(\text{newbalanceDest}\)) are unknown at scoring time. ASTID strictly bans this feature in production scoring.

3.2 Disjunctive Score Aggregator Formulation

Standard linear combinations \(S = w_1 P_{\text{XGB}} + w_2 S_{\text{AE}}\) fail when an extreme zero-day anomaly triggers the autoencoder (\(S_{\text{AE}} = 0.95\)) but supervised XGBoost scores low (\(P_{\text{XGB}} = 0.05\)), yielding \(S = 0.6(0.05) + 0.4(0.95) = 0.41\) (landing in REVIEW instead of escalating). ASTID implements Disjunctive Override Gating:

$$S_{\text{final}} = \begin{cases} 1.00, & \text{if Blacklist Match} \implies \text{BLOCK} \\ 0.95, & \text{if Critical Rule Hit} \implies \text{ESCALATE} \\ \max(0.75, P_{\text{XGB}}), & \text{if } P_{\text{XGB}} \ge 0.85 \implies \text{ESCALATE} \\ \max(0.75, S_{\text{AE}}), & \text{if } S_{\text{AE}} \ge 0.90 \implies \text{ESCALATE} \\ 0.60 P_{\text{XGB}} + 0.40 S_{\text{AE}} + \Delta_{\text{rules}}, & \text{otherwise (Standard Routing)} \end{cases}$$

3.3 Calibrated XGBoost & Deep Bottleneck Autoencoder

Due to extreme class imbalance (\(\pi_1 = 0.129\%\)), XGBoost is cost-reweighted via \(w_{\text{pos}} = \frac{N - N_{\text{pos}}}{N_{\text{pos}}} \approx 773.5\) and calibrated on an untouched validation split using Platt sigmoidal calibration:

$$P(y=1|f) = \frac{1}{1 + \exp(A \cdot f + B)}$$

The unsupervised Autoencoder (\(\text{Linear}(N, 100) \to \text{Linear}(100, 20) \to \text{Linear}(20, 100) \to \text{Linear}(100, N)\)) is trained exclusively on normal transactions (\(y=0\)) using element-wise Mean Squared Error:

$$\text{MSE}(x, \hat{x}) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} (x_i - \hat{x}_i)^2, \quad S_{\text{AE}} = \text{clip}\left(\frac{\text{MSE} - \mu_{\text{train}}}{\tau_{\text{threshold}}}, 0.0, 1.0\right)$$

03 Mathematical Formalisms: FinTech Economics & Governance

3.4 Cost-Sensitive Bayes Dynamic Thresholding (Expected Value Framework)

Standard static thresholds (e.g. \(0.50\)) fail in enterprise banking because financial consequences are asymmetric: missing a large cash-out incurs catastrophic monetary loss, while flagging a small payment causes disproportionate customer friction. ASTID implements Bayes Minimum Risk Dynamic Thresholding, deriving an optimal decision cutoff \(\tau^*(x)\) tailored to transaction amount:

$$\tau^*(x) = \frac{C_{\text{FP}}}{C_{\text{FP}} + C_{\text{FN}}(x)} = \frac{C_{\text{review}} + C_{\text{friction}} + (\mu_{\text{interchange}} \cdot \text{Amount})}{\text{Amount} + C_{\text{chargeback}} + C_{\text{review}} + C_{\text{friction}} + (\mu_{\text{interchange}} \cdot \text{Amount})}$$

Empirical Utility: For a ₹5,00,000 transfer, \(\tau^* \approx 0.007\) (flag on minimal risk); for a ₹150 UPI purchase, \(\tau^* \approx 0.78\) (requires extreme certainty to justify manual review cost).

3.5 Impossible Physical Travel: Spherical Haversine Geo-Velocity

To detect account takeover (ATO) across geographic regions, ASTID computes the great-circle distance between successive transaction coordinates \((\phi_1, \lambda_1)\) and \((\phi_2, \lambda_2)\) using the Haversine Formula:

$$d = 2R \arcsin\left(\sqrt{\sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta \phi}{2}\right) + \cos(\phi_1)\cos(\phi_2)\sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta \lambda}{2}\right)}\right), \quad v = \frac{d}{\Delta t} \quad (\text{km/h})$$

If \(v > 900 \text{ km/h}\) (commercial jet cruising velocity), ASTID flags an impossible travel anomaly (+0.35 risk boost) and generates a Whitebox badge.

3.6 Model Drift Surveillance: Population Stability Index (PSI)

In compliance with Federal Reserve SR Letter 11-7 and Basel II Model Risk Governance, ASTID monitors distribution shift over streaming predictions against frozen validation deciles:

$$\text{PSI} = \sum_{i=1}^{10} \left( \text{Actual}_i - \text{Expected}_i \right) \times \ln\left( \frac{\text{Actual}_i}{\text{Expected}_i} \right)$$

Governance traffic-light: \(\text{PSI} < 0.10 \implies \text{GREEN}\) (stable); \(0.10 \le \text{PSI} < 0.25 \implies \text{AMBER}\) (moderate drift); \(\text{PSI} \ge 0.25 \implies \text{RED}\) (circuit-breaker triggers automated warm-start recalibration).

3.7 Delayed Feedback Modeling & 60-Day Blackout Window

Credit card chargebacks take 30–90 days to settle. Training on recent unconfirmed transactions introduces severe label noise. ASTID partitions retraining streams into a Matured Training Window \([T-365\text{d}, T-60\text{d}]\), a 60-day Observation Blackout Window \([T-60\text{d}, T]\) excluded from supervised training, and a Live Serving Window \([T, \text{now}]\).

4.1 Redis Sliding-Window Velocity State Implementation

To prevent race conditions, ASTID implements sliding-window velocity counters using Redis Sorted Sets (ZSET) with atomic Lua execution: 

-- scripts/sliding_velocity.lua (Atomically evaluated in Redis)
local key = KEYS[1]
local now = tonumber(ARGV[1])
local window = tonumber(ARGV[2]) -- e.g., 3600000 ms (1 hour)
local clear_before = now - window

redis.call('ZREMRANGEBYSCORE', key, '-inf', clear_before)
redis.call('ZADD', key, now, ARGV[3])
local count = redis.call('ZCOUNT', key, clear_before, '+inf')
redis.call('PEXPIRE', key, window * 2)
return count

5.1 3-Tier Row-Level Security (RLS) Specification

-- 1. Helper Functions to prevent recursive RLS queries on users table
CREATE OR REPLACE FUNCTION get_auth_role() RETURNS TEXT AS $$
    SELECT role FROM users WHERE user_id = auth.uid();
$$ LANGUAGE sql STABLE SECURITY DEFINER;

CREATE OR REPLACE FUNCTION get_auth_default_branch() RETURNS UUID AS $$
    SELECT default_branch_id FROM users WHERE user_id = auth.uid();
$$ LANGUAGE sql STABLE SECURITY DEFINER;

CREATE OR REPLACE FUNCTION get_auth_assigned_branches() RETURNS UUID[] AS $$
    SELECT assigned_branch_ids FROM users WHERE user_id = auth.uid();
$$ LANGUAGE sql STABLE SECURITY DEFINER;

-- 2. 3-Tier Role-Based Visibility on transactions table
CREATE POLICY "3-Tier Branch Isolation on Transactions"
ON transactions FOR SELECT TO authenticated USING (
    get_auth_role() IN ('team_lead', 'admin') -- Tier 3: Global All-5-Branches Access
    OR (get_auth_role() = 'senior_analyst' AND branch_id = ANY(get_auth_assigned_branches())) -- Tier 2: Multi-Branch Cluster
    OR (get_auth_role() = 'analyst' AND branch_id = get_auth_default_branch()) -- Tier 1: Own Branch Only
);

-- 3. Dynamic Cron-Free View for 15-Minute SLA Watchdog
CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW v_escalations_with_sla AS
SELECT e.*,
    CASE 
        WHEN e.status = 'open' AND now() > e.sla_deadline THEN 'overdue'
        ELSE e.status 
    END AS live_status
FROM escalations e;

-- 4. Model Drift Governance Logs Table & Policy
CREATE POLICY "Drift logs readable by leadership"
ON model_drift_logs FOR SELECT TO authenticated USING (
    get_auth_role() IN ('team_lead', 'admin')
);

Dhrubojyoti
(Lead & Architect)		 "Why not build a full consumer banking app or SaaS with Stripe integration?" "In enterprise banking, fraud detection operates strictly as internal RiskOps tooling (like Stripe Radar or Feedzai Pulse). Building a consumer payment UI distracts from the core engineering: real-time scoring latency, 3-tier branch access control, human-in-the-loop escalation, and audit compliance."
Isheeka
(Lead ML Engineer)		 "Your early notebook had 1.000 PR-AUC. Why did it drop to 0.884 in the final build?" "The 1.000 score was an artifact of the synthetic PaySim generator (errorBalanceDest), where simulation logic zeroed origin balances. In production, destination post-balances are unknown at scoring time. By isolating that leak and using pre-authorization behavioral features, our 0.884 PR-AUC and 94.2% recall represent genuine, defensible generalization."
Samprikta
(Backend & Scoring)		 "Why not use Apache Kafka or RabbitMQ for streaming payment events?" "Kafka is engineered for horizontal decoupling across hundreds of microservices. At our single-institution scale, Supabase Realtime (PostgreSQL logical replication over WebSockets) delivers sub-150ms push updates with zero JVM maintenance overhead and built-in database consistency."
Adhiraj
(XAI & Simulator)		 "Why combine TreeSHAP with Autoencoder reconstruction error?" "TreeSHAP explains supervised XGBoost feature attributions for known fraud signatures, but supervised models fail on novel zero-day attacks. The Autoencoder flags novel combinations via reconstruction error. Combining both into plain-English Whitebox badges satisfies regulatory compliance while catching zero-days."
Tiyasha
(Warehouse & DB)		 "Why use DuckDB instead of traditional PostgreSQL queries for analytical reporting?" "Row-oriented PostgreSQL requires reading all table columns off disk, choking on 6.36 million rows. DuckDB is a columnar vectorized engine that reads only required analytical columns, executing cross-branch KPI queries in <120 ms directly in memory without database locking."
Samprikta
(Backend & Scoring)		 "How does the system prevent duplicate transactions if an unstable network retries a payment?" "We implement the IETF Idempotency-Key specification with atomic Redis distributed locks (SETNX EX 30). Concurrent retries are caught immediately, and completed runs return cached JSON payloads in <2 ms without re-evaluating ML inference."
Dhrubojyoti
(Lead & Architect)		 "Why not use a standard fixed 0.50 score cutoff across all transaction amounts?" "Fixed thresholds ignore economic reality. Missing a ₹5,00,000 cash-out is catastrophic, while reviewing a ₹150 payment costs more in analyst labor than the transaction itself. Under Bayes Minimum Risk, our dynamic threshold \(\tau^*(x)\) flags high-value transfers at \(\tau^* \approx 0.007\) while avoiding friction on micro-payments."
Tiyasha & Isheeka
(Governance & ML)		 "How do you ensure the model does not silently degrade in production over time?" "Conforming to Federal Reserve SR Letter 11-7 and Basel II Model Risk Governance, ASTID computes the 10-decile Population Stability Index (PSI) and Kolmogorov-Smirnov test over live predictions against frozen training baselines, alerting leadership when drift exceeds 0.10."
